About the college

The School of Information Technology(SOIT)is part of Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya(RGPV),Bhopal. The University's official information states that the college was established in 2002-03 and work in education and research across information technology and related fields.

Vission and Purpose

To nurture academic excellence,innovation,creativity and responsible technology professionals. Refer to the official SOIT page for the institution's complete published vision and mission.

Learning Environment

Academic learning is supported by research,practical work, computing facilities and student participation.
